Telescope and reducer

Seestar S30 / S30 Pro presets select the built-in telephoto camera and native optics automatically. Frames use portrait orientation: S30 ≈ 1.20° × 2.13°; S30 Pro ≈ 2.24° × 3.99°. Compare the two using separate comparison setups.

The Apertura CarbonStar reducing coma corrector is 0.95×. Celestron’s classic SCT reducer is 0.63×; dedicated EdgeHD reducers for the 8, 9.25, 11, and 14 are 0.70×.
